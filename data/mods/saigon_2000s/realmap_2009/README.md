# Sài Gòn, 2009 (real map)

The overmap files for the scenario "Sài Gòn, 2009 (real map)": 5 x 4 overmaps (21.6 x 17.3 km,
24 m per tile) from Tân Sơn Nhất and Gò Vấp to Thủ Đức and Nhà Bè, with the real streets, river,
canals and land use as of late 2009. Overmaps beyond them are generated (Vietmap).
