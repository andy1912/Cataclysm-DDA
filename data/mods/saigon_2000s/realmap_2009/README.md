# Sài Gòn, 2009 (real map)

The overmap files for the scenario "Sài Gòn, 2009 (real map)": 5 x 4 overmaps (21.6 x 17.3 km,
24 m per tile) from Tân Sơn Nhất and Gò Vấp to Thủ Đức and Nhà Bè, with the real streets, river,
canals and land use as of late 2009, at overmaps -1..3 x -2..1. Overmap (0, 0) is central Saigon
(District 1 west, 3, 10), where a new game starts. Overmaps beyond them are generated as the
2009 delta countryside (region sgn_saigon_delta, set by the scenario's overmap_outside_region).
