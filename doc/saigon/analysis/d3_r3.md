## 0. Executive summary

Status since r1, in the main session:
- **B4a has landed** (`b811a25b42`, `531563aa68`) in `data/mods/saigon_2000s/region/{dimension,region_settings,weather}.json`.
- `tests/saigon_weather_test.cpp` `saigon_monsoon_climate` [weather][saigon] advances `calendar::turn` per sample.
- **B6a–c vehicles have landed** in `data/mods/saigon_2000s/vehicles/`.
- **B5e** (loot) has landed.

This revision audits the committed JSON as well as the design.

1. **Weather is cheap.** `update_weather` is called every turn (`src/do_turn.cpp:605`). It regenerates only when `calendar::turn >= nextweather` (`src/weather.cpp:1035`), which is once per weather-type duration. The default is 5 min, so about every 300 turns.
2. **JSON cannot produce Saigon temperatures.**
   - The ±12 K seasonal term and ±6 K noise are C++ constants (`src/weather_gen.cpp:38,42`).
   - The committed B4a generator therefore has a **sawtooth**: daily means run from 19.0 °C on 21 Mar to 35.8 °C on 19 Jun, drop to 25.0 °C, and rise to 36.0 °C on 19 Sep [CALC].
   - **Measured extremes are 5.4–46.4 °C**, with a mean of 27.4 °C (main-session run of `saigon_monsoon_climate`, 3 seeds).
   - **B4b (C++) is therefore a prerequisite for B4c and for any heat-balance tuning.** B4b now also covers the hard-coded New England *water* temperature (`src/weather_gen.cpp:301-330`), which bottoms out at ≈1–3 °C on 15 Nov, is ≈3–5 °C at the scenario's start (21 Dec), and is still only 10–13 °C on 5 Feb.
3. **The monsoon timing works in JSON and is measured.**
   - Wet-season rain falls in 22 % of hours, against 1.4 % in the dry season.
   - At least 50 % of wet-season rain falls in the afternoon.
   - Rain intensity is capped at 3 mm/h (`src/weather.cpp:444-452`).
   - **Findings in the committed JSON** (§2.4c): the wet window over-ran by one day (fixed in the working tree, F3). Dry-season and off-afternoon rain always escalates to heavy `rain`. Dry-season humidity averages ≈59 % with ±60 % noise.
4. **Humidity already raises heat stress** through the apparent-temperature (AT) term (`src/weather.cpp:687-689`). Sweat cooling is a flat 0.008 °C per turn (`src/character_body.cpp:191`). B4c gates sweat by vapour-pressure deficit, behind an option. It is guarded by the hidden `[.bodytemp]` tests (§3.3).
5. **Funnels.** Live filling truncates mm/h to `int` (`src/weather.cpp:334,368`), so the live rates are 0 / 1 / 3 mm/h. Off-bubble back-fill over-credits 2.67× in heavy rain, 4× in light rain, and without bound in very light rain. B4b fixes the signatures and adds a live-vs-back-fill test.
6. **Floods.**
   - Flood-on is a RECURRING EOC with a `condition` on heavy wet-season rain. Both EOCs are pinned to street level (z=0) through `target_var`, so the cost is zero while dry (§4.3).
   - **r3:** flood-off is a lazy 3–48 h drain window that follows the avatar, with an optional `F-drain` loader revert in `map::actualize`. The residual leak is stated.
7. **Vehicles.**
   - There is no lean or balance model beyond "rounded centre of mass inside the wheel hull" (`src/vehicle.cpp:5213-5241`).
   - The ba gác stalls under a one-sided load of at least its own mass. r2 keeps this as a mechanic and adds a test.
   - The xích lô and the ghe/long-tail are **pass-1 reskins**. `PROTRUSION` on a non-structure part is a no-op.
   - Each vehicle is tied to the pass-2 seam that will make it distinct (§5).
8. **Monsters.**
   - `GROUP_ZOMBIE_HORDE` is only **2.4 %** dormant (250 of 10,477).
   - Raising `SPAWN_CITY_HORDE_SCALAR` therefore adds active zombies. Dormant density must come from explicit mapgen groups.
   - The budget in §6.2 is rebuilt on that basis.

### 1.2 What `weather_manager::update_weather` does (`src/weather.cpp:1031-1077`)

- **Gate.** The body runs only if `weather_id == WEATHER_NULL || calendar::turn >= nextweather` (`:1035`).
- **Regenerate the state.** `w = weather_gen.get_weather( player_character.pos_abs(), calendar::turn, g->get_seed() )` (`:1038`).
  - Temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed and wind direction are all recomputed **together**, at the player's position only.
  - The weather is one global state for the whole bubble.
- **Select the weather type.** `weather_id = weather_gen.get_weather_conditions( w )` (`:1045`).
  - The selector (`src/weather_gen.cpp:237-270`) swaps `weather_precise` in and builds one `dialogue`.
  - It walks `sorted_weather` in `priority` order and evaluates each type's JSON `condition`, gated by `required_weathers`.
  - The last type that matches wins (`:261-263`).
- **Schedule the next change.** `nextweather = turn + rng( duration_min, duration_max )` (`:1056`).
  - The defaults are 5 min each (`src/weather_type.cpp:129-130`).
  - `grep -c duration_min data/json/weather_type.json` returns 0, so dda regenerates the weather **every 300 turns**.
  - The committed Saigon `thunder` sets 40 min–2 h (`data/mods/saigon_2000s/region/weather.json:103-104`), so storms hold longer and regenerations are rarer.
- **On a type change** (`:1067-1074`):
  - transparency caches are dirtied on all 21 z-levels, but only if `sight_penalty` differs;
  - `effect_on_conditions::process_reactivate()` runs, which re-queues inactive global EOCs (`src/effect_on_condition.cpp:344-349`, `:320-336`).
- **Every call.** `update_snow_depth()` (`:1076`) is itself gated to once per minute (`:1099`).

### 1.3 Other per-turn weather consumers

- `weather_manager::get_temperature( tripoint_bub_ms )` (`src/weather.cpp:1192-1219`):
  - memoised per location per turn in `temperature_cache`;
  - adds `get_heat_radiation`, `get_convection_temperature`, `map::get_temperature_mod` and `weather_type::temperature_modifier`.
- `handle_weather_effects` (`src/weather.cpp:455-489`). While it rains:
  - `fill_water_collectors` walks every funnel trap location (`:334-366`, `:368-374`);
  - `map::decay_fields_and_scent` decays the whole 132×132 scent grid (`src/scent_map.cpp:59-66`) and the outside fields of submaps that have fields (`src/map.cpp:3907-3950`).
  - Separately, `for( Creature &c : g->all_creatures() ) maybe_apply_field_effect( w->passive_effect, c )` (`src/weather.cpp:484`) runs **every turn for every creature**. `maybe_apply_field_effect` does a `veh_at` before it looks at the (usually empty) effect list (`src/map_field.cpp:1783-1795`). In a dense zombie city this is N `veh_at` lookups per turn for nothing (see pass 2, P2-0).
- Off-bubble consumers that call the **generator** directly, not the cached state:
  - item rot: `wgen.get_weather_temperature` (`src/item_degrade.cpp:1372`);
  - mapgen: `src/mapgen.cpp:6482`;
  - the forecast: `src/weather.cpp:583-585`;
  - planting: `highest_temp_on_day` (`src/weather.cpp:893-905`);
  - retroactive funnel fill: `sum_conditions` → `current_weather` → `get_weather_conditions( location, t, seed )` (`src/weather.cpp:192-231`).

  So an EOC that assigns `weather('temperature')` (`weather_ass`, `src/math_parser_diag.cpp:1828-1845`) would **not** change item rot or forecasts. This rules out an "EOC temperature clamp" for B4a.

### 1.4 Cost

- **Per turn, fixed:**
  - `temperature_cache.clear()`;
  - one comparison of `nextweather`;
  - the per-minute `update_snow_depth` loop over tracked OMTs;
  - during rain, one pass over 17,424 scent ints (`src/scent_map.cpp:61-65`);
  - N `veh_at` lookups (`src/weather.cpp:484`).

## 2. Q2: Can JSON alone express the Saigon climate?

### 2.1 The temperature formula (quoted, `src/weather_gen.cpp:145-152`)

```cpp
const double baseline(
    wg.base_temperature +
    seasonal_temp_mod[season] +
    dayv * ( daily_magnitude_K + daily_seasonal_range_K * ( -seasonality + 1 ) / 2 ) +
    seasonality * seasonality_magnitude_K );
const double T = baseline + raw_noise_4d( x, y, z, modSEED ) *
                 ( 1 + ( 1 + -seasonality ) * seasonal_noise_magnitude / 2 ) * noise_magnitude_K;
```

**Hard-coded constants** (`src/weather_gen.cpp:31-44`), none of which can be set from JSON:

| Constant | Value | Line |
|---|---|---|
| `coldest_hour` | 5 | `:31` |
| `daily_magnitude_K` | 5 | `:33` |
| `daily_seasonal_range_K` | 2 | `:36` |
| `seasonality_magnitude_K` | **12** | `:38` |
| `noise_magnitude_K` | **6** | `:42` |
| `seasonal_noise_magnitude` | 0.5 | `:44` |

**Seasonality term:**
- `seasonality = -cyf`, where `cyf = cos( tau * ( year_fraction - offset ) )` (`src/weather_gen.cpp:109-119,133`).
- The offset puts midwinter on turn-day 318.5 (≈5 Feb) and midsummer on **turn-day 136.5 (≈5–6 Aug)**, counting from turn 0 = 21 Mar (`src/calendar.cpp:856-866`). CDDA months are Jan 31, Feb 30, Mar 30, Apr 31, May 30, Jun 30, Jul 31, Aug 30, Sep 30, Oct 31, Nov 30 and Dec 30 days (`month_and_day`, `src/calendar.cpp:876-910`) [CALC].

**Seasonal modifiers.** `seasonal_temp_mod[season]` is indexed by `season_of_year` (`src/weather_gen.cpp:141-147`). A season is `turn / season_length % 4` (`src/calendar.cpp:814-832`).

**JSON keys** (`src/weather_gen.cpp:389-409`):
- mandatory: `base_temperature`, `base_humidity`, `base_pressure`, `base_wind`;
- optional: `base_wind_distrib_peaks`, `base_wind_season_variation`, `{spring,summer,autumn,winter}_temp_manual_mod`, `{…}_humidity_manual_mod`, and either `weather_black_list` or `weather_white_list` (never both, `:407-408`).

### 2.2 Humidity and pressure (`src/weather_gen.cpp:184-205`)

- **Humidity:**
  `H = clamp( base_humidity + mod_h[season] + 100*( .15*seasonality + noise*.2*( 2 - seasonality ) ), 0, 100 )`.
  - The seasonal cosine adds ±15 %.
  - The noise amplitude is ±20 % in midsummer and **±60 % in midwinter**. Saigon's dry-season target is 65–75 %.
- **Pressure:** `P = base_pressure + noise*15*( 2 - seasonality )`.
  - That is ±15 hPa in summer and ±45 hPa in winter.
  - dda rain is triggered by `pressure < 1003/1000/993` or `humidity >= 96/97/98` (`data/json/weather_type.json:42-110`). So vanilla rain is **more** likely in winter. That is the reverse of a monsoon.

### 2.3 What JSON can and cannot do [CALC]

**Model.** `wx.py` evaluates the formula above with `SEASON_LENGTH` = 91.

| Target (Bible §9) | JSON-only? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mean 27–28 °C | yes, via `base_temperature` | `:146` |
| Annual range ~3 °C | **no** | `12·seasonality` spans 16.8 K *inside* spring and inside autumn. The best per-season offsets leave a ±8.4 K residual and ≈10.6 K jumps at every season boundary. |
| No frost | **no** | Noise adds ±6–9 K on top (`:151-152`). **Measured with the committed B4a generator: minimum 5.4–6.4 °C and maximum 45.4–46.4 °C over 3 seeds** (main-session and critic runs of `saigon_monsoon_climate`). |
| April peak of 35 °C | no, because the cosine peaks on 5–6 Aug | `:116-119` |
| Humidity 65–75 % in the dry season | no: noise is ±60 % in midwinter | `:195-199` |
| Humidity 80–90 % in the wet season | roughly: base 70 + summer/autumn mods, noise ±20–30 % | `:195-199` |

- A hot body is kept at wetness ≥ 1 (`:196-203`).
- Humidity only slows **drying** (`weather_mult`, `:143-147`). That *prolongs* the constant cooling. So high humidity currently **helps** evaporative cooling, which is physically backwards.

**Thirst from heat.** This lives in JSON effect `hot`: `thirst_min 1 / thirst_tick 60 / thirst_chance 6`, scaling `thirst_min +1`, plus perspiration (`data/json/effects.json:3306-3336`). It is applied through `Character::process_effects` "THIRST"/"PERSPIRATION" (`src/character.cpp:6724-6746`).

**Equilibrium [CALC].**
- Convergence plus the fixed sweat gives `temp_cur ≈ temp_conv − 4·clothing_mult`, because `0.002·Δ = 0.008`.

| Conditions (lightly clothed) | `temp_conv` | `temp_cur` |
|---|---|---|
| Outdoors in the sun, 33 °C, RH 70 %, wind 5 mph | 45.0 | ≈41.0 (`hot` level 1) |
| Shade, RH 50 % | 40.8 | 36.8 |
| Shade, RH 90 % | 43.2 | 39.2 |
| Indoors (sheltered) at 30 °C, RH 80 %, no wind | 42.6 | 38.6 |

- **Conclusion:** humidity already drives heat stress through the AT term, by 2.4 °C of `temp_conv` between RH 50 % and 90 %. What is missing is the physiological core, *reduced sweat efficiency*.

### 3.3 B4c (C++): humidity-limited sweat cooling (**GO behind an option; blocked on B4b**)

**Prerequisite.** B4b must land first. With the B4a sawtooth, a humid late-June day at 36 °C plus B4c reaches `hot` 3 (critic D2).

**Seam.**
- `update_bodytemp` already computes `sheltered` (`src/character_body.cpp:439`) and the local humidity it feeds to AT (`:538-540`). Compute `evap_eff` there, once per call, and cache it in a new `Character` member `sweat_evap_eff`.
- `update_body_wetness` runs right after it in both call sites (`src/do_turn.cpp:767-768`; `npc::update_bodytemp_and_wetness`, `src/npc.cpp:3530-3537`). It multiplies the cooling term at `src/character_body.cpp:191` by `sweat_evap_eff`.
- This avoids a new signature. `update_body_wetness( const w_point & )` has neither shelter nor `weather_id` (critic D-m3).
- It also avoids an extra `veh_at`. `game::is_sheltered` calls `here->veh_at( p )` (`src/game.cpp:4921-4929`), and `update_bodytemp` already pays for it twice, at `:439` and `:480`. r1's "`veh_at`-free" claim was wrong.

**Cache rules (r3, critic D-m6).**
- `float sweat_evap_eff = 1.0f;` is a transient member. It is **not serialised**: not added to `Character::store`/`load`. It defaults to 1.0, so after a load or for a new character the vanilla behaviour holds until the next `update_bodytemp`.
- Reset it to 1.0 on both early returns of `update_bodytemp`: `DEBUG_NOTEMP` and `CANNOT_CHANGE_TEMPERATURE` (`src/character_body.cpp:420-426`).
- Reset it to 1.0 when the option is off, so the multiply is an exact no-op (× 1.0f) and the off path is numerically identical.
- **`drying_rate` path.** `tests/limb_test.cpp:220,243` call `update_body_wetness` in a loop **without** `update_bodytemp`. With the default 1.0 the test sees vanilla cooling, so it is unaffected even with the option on.
- **Option read.** Read `HUMID_SWEAT_MODEL` once per turn into a static cached by `options_manager::update_options_cache` (the same mechanism used after external options load, `src/worldfactory.cpp:2091`). Do not call `get_option<bool>` per character per turn.

**Formula.**
- `e_skin = 6.105·exp(17.27·35/(237.7+35)) = 56.0 hPa`.
- `e_air = RH_local/100 · 6.105·exp(17.27·T/(237.7+T))`, using `get_local_humidity( weather.humidity, weather_id, sheltered )`.
- `evap_eff = clamp( (e_skin − e_air)/e_skin, 0.25, 1.0 )`.

## 4. Q4: Water, rain collection and flooding

### 4.1 How rain feeds containers today

**Trap funnels** (live, in the bubble):
- Flow: `handle_weather_effects` → `fill_water_collectors( precip_mm_per_hour( w->precip ) )` (`src/weather.cpp:461`) → `fill_funnels` (`:334-366`).
- **Both take `int` mm/h** (`:334,368`), so the 0.5/1.5/3 mm/h class rates become **0/1/3**. Very light rain never fills a funnel (§2.6).
- Per turn, each funnel location has `one_in( turns_per_charge )` of dropping 1 charge (250 ml) into the largest `is_funnel_container` item on the tile (`:342-364`).
- `funnel_charges_per_turn` is `area·mm/h / 3600 / 250 000` (`:283-307`).
- Funnel radii in JSON (`funnel_radius`, `data/json/traps.json`):

  | Trap | Radius (mm) | Line |
  |---|---|---|
  | `tr_funnel` | 300 | `:59` |
  | `tr_makeshift_funnel` | 200 | `:73` |
  | `tr_downspout_funnel` | 1200 | `:881` |
  | `tr_metal_funnel` | 400 | `:895` |
  | `tr_raincatcher` | 342 | `:958` |

- Yield at the heavy cap of 3 mm/h [CALC]. Light rain counts as 1 mm/h, a third of these figures:
  - `tr_funnel`: 0.85 L/h;
  - downspout: 13.6 L/h.

**Off-bubble funnels** are back-filled by `retroactively_fill_from_funnel` (`src/weather.cpp:233-250`). It over-credits ∞ / 4× / 2.67× for very light / light / heavy rain (§2.6).

**Vehicle funnels:**
- `FUNNEL` parts are cached (`src/vehicle.cpp:6937-6938`).
- `vehicle::update_time` runs at most once per minute (`:8611-8614`), calls `sum_conditions`, and fills a water tank on the same mount (`:8619-8660`).
- There are five `FUNNEL` vehicle parts (`data/json/vehicleparts/vehicle_parts.json:2810-2894`).

**Other rain effects:**
- Rain wets the character (`wet_character`, `src/weather.cpp:387-419`), unless roofed (`:481-482`).
- Rain decays scent and outdoor fields (`:474`).
- **Weather masks sound** (a missed topic in r1). `weather_type::sound_attn` is subtracted from every sound before monsters hear it (`src/sounds.cpp:451,478,577`) and from the player's hearing (`src/character_health.cpp:3109`). In dda JSON it is 1 for drizzle, 4 for rain and 8 for rainstorm, thunder and lightning (`data/json/weather_type.json:76,99,122,147,170`).
  - The committed B4a overrides use `copy-from`, so they inherit these values.
  - Attenuation **by type** (corrects the r2 overstatement, critic D-m11):

    | Weather type | `sound_attn` |
    |---|---|
    | `light_drizzle` (the very-light root) | 0 |
    | `drizzle` | 1 |
    | `rain` | 4 |
    | `thunder`, `lightning`, `rainstorm` | 8 |

  - `sound_attn` is global, applied to every sound including indoor and underground ones (`src/sounds.cpp:451,478,577`).
  - So the strong stealth window is the **thunder afternoon**. Ordinary rain gives a modest −4, and drizzle almost nothing. Scent wash-out (`:474`) happens in any rain of `light` class or heavier.

### 4.2 Does rain collect on terrain? Floods? Tides?

- **No.** The terrain never changes because of rain. The only precipitation state is snow depth per OMT (`src/weather.cpp:1097-1164`).
- There is no puddle field spawned by rain. `fd_dirty_water` exists only as a furniture-destroy field (`data/json/fields/debris.json:60-74`; used at `data/json/furniture_and_terrain/furniture-plumbing.json:27`).

It is a buffered cellular automaton over one z-level: `t_rock_floor` next to water becomes `t_water_sh`, and `t_water_sh` next to deep water becomes `t_water_dp`. It re-schedules itself (`:283`). Cost: 2 × 17,424 `ter()` reads per step.

**r3 drain redesign (critic D1).**
- r2's flood-off cleared `sgn_flooded` on its first firing. So it drained only the 60-tile disc around the avatar *at that moment*, and every disc flooded elsewhere during the storm stayed flooded for good.
- In r3, flood-off keeps `sgn_flooded = 1` and re-fires every 30 min from 3 h to 48 h after the last heavy rain. It drains around wherever the avatar is each time. The flag is cleared only when the 48 h window closes (an inline `run_eocs` with its own `condition`).
- A storm during the window restarts it, because flood-on refreshes `sgn_last_heavy_rain`.
- Cost while draining: one 14,641-tile transform per 30 min for ≤ 45 h, i.e. ≤ 90 activations per storm. Most are no-ops on tiles that are already dry. UNVERIFIED; B5d times it.

**Residual leak (stated).**
- A flooded disc the player never comes within 60 tiles of during the 45 h window stays flooded until a later drain window passes near it.
- In the wet season that is the next storm, often within days.
- Late in the season, a tile can stay flooded through the dry season. That is plausible for a low-lying hẻm, but unintended.

**Loader-side revert (seam `F-drain`, ~12 LOC C++, recommended with B4b).**

### 4.5 Item temperature, ice and the thùng xốp (missed topic in r1)

**Model** [V]:
- `item::process_temperature_rot` (`src/item_degrade.cpp:1286`) updates item temperature at most every 10 min.
- For elapsed time, it walks hourly steps using the **generator** temperature off-bubble (`:1365-1373`), and calls `calc_temp` only for the last 2 days (`:1399-1401`).
- `item::calc_temp` (`src/item.cpp:4203`) is Newton cooling with `conductivity_term = 0.0076·V_ml^(2/3)/insulation` (`:4224-4225`), plus a latent-heat plateau at the freezing point.
- Water ice uses the `water` material: `latent_heat` 333 J/g and `specific_heat_solid` 2.108 (`data/json/materials.json:2740-2745`).
- A pocket's `insulation` propagates to its contents as `max( pocket.insulation(), insulation )` (`src/item_contents.cpp:2771`).
- The committed cooler `sgn_thung_xop` has pocket `insulation` 4 (`data/mods/saigon_2000s/items/gear/gear.json:102-124`).

| Block | Bare (insulation 1) | In `sgn_thung_xop` (4) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 L (1 kg) | 4.1 h | **16.2 h** |
| 5 L (5 kg) | 6.9 h | **27.7 h** |

**Consequences.**
- At 35 °C the times drop by a factor of 30/35.
- Saigon never drops below 0 °C, so water cannot freeze in the world. Ice exists only where mapgen or loot places frozen water, or from a powered freezer.
- So a block of ice is a perishable that lasts about a day in a styrofoam box. That is the Bible's cooler gameplay, from existing code.
- **Coupling to B4b:** off-bubble catch-up uses the generator temperature, so the B4a sawtooth (36 °C means in June and September) shortens ice and food life in those weeks. B4b removes this.

### 5.d Two- and three-wheel physics: what exists, and the pass-2 seams

**What exists** [V]:
- **Wheel count.** `sufficient_wheel_config` (`src/vehicle.cpp:5093-5106`): ≥ 2 wheels, or 1 `STABLE` wheel on ≤ 3 structure tiles. Every wheel must declare `UNSTABLE_WHEEL` or `STABLE` (`src/veh_type.cpp:897-903`).
- **Balance** means only that the rounded COM lies inside the convex hull of the wheel mounts (`src/vehicle.cpp:5213-5241`). An inline two-wheeler always passes, because both angles are 0 and π and the gap is exactly π.
  - Passengers add mass to the COM (`src/vehicle.cpp:8841-8846`) but cannot tip a one-tile-wide bike.
  - There is **no lean, tip-over, low-speed instability or kickstand state** in motion.
- **Handling.**
  - `handling_difficulty = velocity·(3 − steer − ktraction − aligned)/vmiph_per_tile` (`src/vehicle.cpp:5300-5314`).
  - Turning cost and fumble chance come from `pldrive` (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:1599-1733`).
  - Skids start from thick ice (`:499-514`), collision Δv (`:1263-1269`), or losing control (`:2111-2116`).
  - **Rain does not reduce traction.** `map::vehicle_wheel_traction` knows only terrain modifiers and `THICK_ICE` (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:2307-2370`).
- **Traffic.** Vehicles occupy whole tiles. `vehicle::collision` and `part_collision` (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:745,879`) treat any overlap with another vehicle as a crash. So a 1-wide bike already "lane-splits" through a free 1-tile gap between wrecks, and cannot share a tile.

**Pass-2 seams (P2-4).** Ordered by value and risk:
1. **Wet-road traction.**
   - Where: in both `vehicle_wheel_traction` overloads (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:2361-2366` and the twin at `:2373+`).
   - What: multiply the contribution by `wet_factor` (0.7) when `get_weather().weather_id->rains && precip >= light` and the tile is outside (`map::is_outside`). Gate it with a new wheel JSON key `wet_traction` (default 1.0) in `veh_type.cpp` wheel loading.
   - Cost: one cached weather read per call.
   - Test: in `tests/vehicle_test.cpp`, force `rain` and assert `k_traction` drops on `t_pavement` for `sgn_veh_underbone`.
2. **Two-wheeler tip-over.**
   - Where: a new vehicle flag `TWO_WHEEL`, derived in `vehicle::refresh` when the wheel cache has 2 wheels on one y (`src/vehicle.cpp:6935-6960`).
   - What: in `pldrive` (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:1680-1702`), when `TWO_WHEEL`, a fumble **or** a skid at under 10 mph rolls a Dex + driving check. On failure: `velocity = 0`, the rider and pillion are thrown (`vehicle::throw_off_passengers` style via `get_passenger`), a `downed` effect, and a new `vehicle` bool `tipped` that blocks `thrust` until the player "rights" the bike (an `examine` action).

  - width uses `tile_to_width`, so 1 tile = 0.9 m and 2 tiles = 1.3 m (`:4556-4564`).
- **Water detection** (`check_falling_or_floating`, `src/vehicle_move.cpp:2231-2305`):
  - `in_water` if ≥ ½ of the tiles are `SWIMMABLE`;
  - `in_deep_water` if ≥ ⅔ are `DEEP_WATER`.
  - `is_watercraft = in_deep_water || ( in_water && wheelcache.empty() )` (`src/vehicle.cpp:4887-4890`).
- **So wheel-less boats navigate *shallow* `t_water_murky`.** It has flags `SWIMMABLE` and `SHALLOW_WATER`, not `DEEP_WATER` (`data/json/furniture_and_terrain/terrain-liquids.json:79-89`).
  - `thrust` skips the wheel check for floating watercraft (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:473-475`).
  - Traction and steering become 1.0 (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:2309-2314`; `src/vehicle.cpp:5258-5261`).
- **At the bank:** once more than half of the hull is on land, `is_watercraft` turns false and `thrust` stops with "not enough wheels" (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:482-487`). This is a natural beaching behaviour.
- **Quirks:**
  - In shallow canals the land `prof_driver` applies, not `prof_boat_pilot`, because the check is on `in_deep_water` (`src/vehicle_move.cpp:1606-1611`).
  - The `CURRENT` terrain flag does not push boats; it is only read for water wheels (`src/vehicle.cpp:8572-8573`).
- **Mapgen:** a boat placed with `place_vehicles` needs more than 2 `FLOATS` parts to count as floating (`src/mapgen.cpp:6940`). Only non-floaters are rejected on `DEEP_WATER` (`d2_r3`).

**Engines on water.**
- Any `ENGINE` gives thrust. No propeller part exists or is needed. Precedent: `boat_motor_single` uses `engine_1cyl_large` on a `metal_boat_hull` (`data/json/vehicles/boats.json:182-212`).
- Muscle: `hand_paddles` 720 W, `MUSCLE_ARMS`, `CONTROLS` (`data/json/vehicleparts/manual.json:56-75`). dda has no "oars" part.
- Canoe water drag, safe and max speed: 2.19, 2.7 mph and 5.7 mph (`data/mods/TEST_DATA/vehicle_drag_test.json:52`).

## 6. Q6: Zombie density and the per-turn budget for a hyper-dense city

### 6.1 Where monsters come from [V]

**1. Mapgen `place_monsters` / `place_monster` → `map::place_spawns`** (`src/mapgen.cpp:6564-6620`):
- `num = roll_remainder( density × SPAWN_DENSITY × rng_float( 10, 50 ) )` (`:6583-6593`).
- `density` is the entry's `density` key, or `dat.monster_density()` when it is absent (`jmapgen_monster_group::apply`, `src/mapgen.cpp:2525-2545`).
- `monster_density` is Σ `mondensity` over the 7×7 OMT neighbourhood, divided by 100:
  - loop at `src/mapgen.cpp:248-254`, `MON_RADIUS = 3` at `:163`;
  - `mondensity` loaded at `src/overmap_terrain.cpp:588`, default 0.
- dda city buildings get `mondensity: 2` from the `generic_city_building_no_sidewalk` abstract (`data/json/overmap/overmap_terrain/overmap_terrain.json:38-44`). `road` has none (`data/json/overmap/overmap_terrain/overmap_terrain_transportation.json:4-21`).
- So a solid city block gives a density of ≤ 0.98, and each un-densitied `GROUP_ZOMBIE` entry yields about 10–49 group draws before group costs. Examples: `data/json/mapgen/house/house01.json:104`, `data/json/mapgen/abstorefront.json:258`.
- Static OMT `spawns` are applied in `map::generate` (`src/mapgen.cpp:306-330`).

**2. Overmap city hordes** (`overmap::place_mongroups`, `src/overmap.cpp:3444-3525`):
- `desired_zombies = city.size × SPAWN_CITY_HORDE_SCALAR (80) × SPAWN_DENSITY` (`:3459`).
- They are spread within `size × SPAWN_CITY_HORDE_SPREAD (1.5)` OMT, **only on OMTs of type `road`** (`:3479`), with 1–4 submaps per road OMT by distance (`:3493-3498`), in `GROUP_ZOMBIE_HORDE` chunks of ≤ 10 (`:3516-3522`).
- The options are `EXTERNAL_OPTION`s (`data/core/external_options.json:179-203`), so a mod can override them. `SPAWN_DENSITY` is a world option (`src/options.cpp:2852`).
- **`GROUP_ZOMBIE_HORDE` is only 2.4 % dormant** (corrects r1).
  - Its 46 entries sum to weight 10,477, of which `GROUP_VANILLA_DORMANT` is 250 (`data/json/monstergroups/zombies.json:94-147`). `GROUP_ZOMBIE` is 250/10,977 = 2.3 % (`:148+`) [SCRIPT].
  - `horde_map::spawn_entity` files non-DORMANT types into the idle or immobile maps (`src/horde_map.cpp:136-146`).
  - So raising `SPAWN_CITY_HORDE_SCALAR` adds **active** zombies almost one for one.
- **Horde gating.** City hordes are placed only if `city.size > SPAWN_CITY_HORDE_THRESHOLD` (4) or `one_in( SPAWN_CITY_HORDE_SMALL_CITY_CHANCE )` (16) (`src/overmap.cpp:3447-3455`; `data/core/external_options.json:179-189`).
  - `d2_r3` plans `city_size` 12–14, so the district city always gets hordes: 960–1,120 entities at scalar 80.
  - Small satellite towns in the Saigon region get none 15 times in 16.
- **Dormant zombies are cheap.** They are corpses plus `tr_dormant_corpse` traps. `item::process_corpse` only re-checks the trap (`src/item.cpp:4452-4468`), and the horde map files them in `dormant_monster_map` (`src/horde_map.cpp:136-146`). They wake on trigger.

**3. Materialisation.**
- `map::spawn_monsters` walks every submap on every z (`src/map.cpp:10237-10252`) → `spawn_monsters_submap(_group)` (`:10016,10163`).
- `overmapbuffer::spawn_monster` extracts horde entities per submap (`src/overmapbuffer.cpp:2129-2175`).

**4. Per turn.**
- `monmove()` (`src/do_turn.cpp:279-360`) walks `g->all_monsters()` with **no cap**. Each monster runs `process_turn`, then `plan()` + `move()` while it has moves (`:316-341`; `monster::plan` at `src/monmove.cpp:558`, `monster::move` at `:976`).
- `mon_info_update()` runs up to 3 times per turn (`src/do_turn.cpp:642,692,749`).
- `overmap_buffer.move_hordes()` runs every turn over overmaps within `MAPSIZE*2` submaps (`src/overmapbuffer.cpp:712-719`). It walks every **active** horde entity (`src/overmap.cpp:1559-1620`).
- `handle_weather_effects` does one `veh_at` per creature per turn (`src/weather.cpp:484`).
