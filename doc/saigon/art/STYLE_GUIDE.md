# Saigon 2000s art style guide

This guide covers every sprite the Saigon mod ships. The list of what to draw, and in which order, is in
[`manifest.csv`](manifest.csv), generated by `tools/saigon/art_manifest.py`. How sources become a tileset is in
[`PIPELINE.md`](PIPELINE.md). Setting canon is in [`../CONTENT_BIBLE.md`](../CONTENT_BIBLE.md).

## 1. One source, two projections
Every asset is **modelled once** as simple 3D geometry in Blender, then rendered by script into:

| Output | Used by | Projection | Base cell | Per sprite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top-down** | Today's renderer (pass 1) | Orthographic, camera straight down, slight 15° tilt only for tall furniture "front faces" | 32×32 px per tile | 1 view; walls use 16 connection variants |
| **Isometric** | Renderer roadmap phase R3, the iso projection | 2:1 dimetric: camera azimuth 45°, elevation 30°, orthographic | 64×32 px floor diamond; tall objects extend upward (e.g. 64×96) | 4 facings (0/90/180/270°); walls 2 faces |
| **Normal + height + emissive maps** | Renderer roadmap phase R4, per-pixel lighting | Same camera as the iso colour pass | Same as iso | Tangent-space normals, 8-bit height, emissive mask |

Rules:
- Scale is **1 tile = 1 m = 1 Blender unit**. A nhà ống lot is 4 m wide. The underbone is 3 tiles long, with one part per tile.
- The colour pass is near-albedo. Bake in soft ambient occlusion but **no hard directional shadows**. Dynamic lighting (R4) adds shadows later, and baked-in ones would double up.
- Pivot: the floor centre of the tile. Tall objects grow up and back in iso, never forward past the tile.
- Vehicle parts are rendered per part, not per vehicle, because the engine assembles vehicles from parts.

## 2. Palette and mood
- **Light.** Hard tropical sun, but bake neutral light. Sun mood comes from the renderer's colour grading, not the sprites.
- **Façades.** Sun-bleached pastels: ochre, butter yellow, pale green, salmon, faded blue. Streaked with rain stains and AC drips. Hand-painted shop signs: generic words only, never real brands.
- **Metal.** Teal or green rolling shutters (cửa cuốn) and black iron accordion gates (cửa sắt kéo). Window bars everywhere.
- **Accents.** Red and gold altar lacquer. Red plastic stools. Blue tarps. Orange water jars. Green tropical foliage: banana, bougainvillea, potted plants on every ledge.
- **Ground.** Patched grey asphalt. Square sidewalk tiles. Wet sheen variants for rain, needed by flood/weather states.
- **Night.** Sodium-orange street lamps, cold fluorescent tubes in shops, and red altar bulbs. These are emissive masks; per the d1 emitter rule, at most one lit fixture per OMT on average.

## 3. Period and canon rules (2004)
- Phones are Nokia-style bricks; no smartphones. TVs are CRTs. Internet cafés have beige PCs.
- Motorbikes are underbones and simple scooters, with **no logos or real model names** (brand rule, Bible §0).
- Signage uses Vietnamese words with diacritics, shown only as stylised scribbles at 32 px. Never readable real-brand text.
- No wartime iconography, uniforms or flags (tone rule). Ward police wear khaki, never military camouflage.
- Never exotic: everyday objects drawn with care, not tourist postcards.

## 4. Readability at 32 px
Each variant must read by **silhouette plus one colour cue**:

| Sprite | Cue |
|---|---|
| Helmeted zombie (`sgn_mon_zombie_xe_om`) | Half-helmet dome plus visor strap |
| Market zombie (`sgn_mon_zombie_vendor`) | Bulk plus rubber apron (orange) |
| Ward police zombie (`sgn_mon_zombie_cong_an`) | Khaki shirt plus peaked cap |
| Fevered dead (`sgn_mon_zombie_sot`) | Pale gown plus white mask, flushed skin |
| Nón lá | Unmistakable cone, even at 8 px |
| Underbone vs scooter | Underbone: open step-through with a visible frame tube. Scooter: closed body panels |

## 5. Terrain connection
Walls, railings and the sidewalk-to-road kerb follow the base tileset's multitile conventions: the `center`, `corner`, `edge`, `t_connection`, `end_piece` and `unconnected` subtiles in `doc/TILESET.md`. Roof decks need an edge variant against open air, so players can see the drop.

## 6. Priorities (from the manifest)
1. **T1, 193 sprites:** everything on screen in every district. Do these first, in both projections.
2. **T2, 60 sprites:** Saigon items and loot.
3. **T3, 2,365 sprites:** deep dda loot. Reuse base-tileset sprites, and restyle only where they look American.

## 7. Provenance and licence
- Every sprite has a `SOURCES` entry: author, tool, date and licence (CC-BY-SA 3.0, matching the community tilesets).
- AI-assisted art is allowed only if its licence is clear and recorded, and only after a human cleanup pass for palette and silhouette consistency.
- Sources (`.blend`, layered files) live in a separate art repository, or in Git LFS, never in the game repo. Only the composed sprite sheets ship in the mod.
